Reverse engineer anything in your pocket. Crémux is built on Termux: decompile, patch, rebuild and ship APKs without a PC.

* REAPER: one-tap jadx, apktool, radare2 and Ghidra with a live log.
* Smart decompiling: detects packers, Flutter, React Native, Unity and split bundles, then retries with safer settings.
* AI assistant: OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama. Every command needs your approval.
* Rebuild & Sign: rebuild and re-sign apktool projects on the phone.
* GitHub Sync: push, watch the workflow, download the APK for your ABI.
* Zip and share: Deflate-9 zips with split parts.
* Full Termux underneath: shells, packages, SSH, Git, compilers.

Use only on software you own or are authorized to analyze. Not affiliated with the Termux project.
